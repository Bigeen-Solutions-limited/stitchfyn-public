How it works

From fabric drop-off to collection — every step on record

  1. 1

    Intake, photographed

    Photograph the fabric when it arrives. The customer confirms it from their own phone — no app to install. A Fabric Safety Seal is issued the moment they confirm.

  2. 2

    Three checks before a single cut

    Production cannot start until the deposit is recorded, the fabric is confirmed and the design brief is agreed. No more "that's not what I asked for".

  3. 3

    Production, one tap per stage

    Cutting, sewing, fitting, finishing — your team moves each garment forward in seconds. Quality control needs a photo before it can be signed off.

  4. 4

    Collection, with proof

    A final quality review, then collection is recorded. Your customer followed every stage on their tracking link, so nobody is surprised on the day.

What StitchFYN actually does

You do not need more software. You need less chaos.

Fabric is never in dispute again

Every fabric is photographed at intake — one tap, under 30 seconds. The customer confirms from their own phone. The record cannot be edited afterwards. The chain of custody begins before a single stitch is cut.

StitchFYN shows you which orders are most urgent and flags the ones at risk of missing their date. Capacity limits — with seasonal overrides for festive peaks — warn you before you accept one order too many.

Questions tailors ask us

How much does it cost?

Nothing during the founding period. No card is needed to apply. Before the founding period ends, we will tell every Founding Tailor what pricing looks like — well in advance, and never as a surprise.

Do I need a computer?

No. StitchFYN runs in the browser on any modern smartphone, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. A laptop works too, if you have one.

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. Customers confirm their fabric and follow their order through a private link. That is all they need.

What happens when my network is bad?

Stage updates and fabric photos are saved on your phone and sent automatically when your connection comes back. You can keep working.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. You invite your managers and tailors, and each person gets access that matches their role. Every action is recorded against the person who took it.

Why can't I just sign up now?

Every Founding Tailor workshop is set up personally by our team, so your shop, staff and first orders are right from day one. Apply below and we will reach you on WhatsApp.

Who can see my records?

Only your workshop. Each business's orders, customers and evidence are kept separate from every other business on StitchFYN. A customer sees only their own order.
