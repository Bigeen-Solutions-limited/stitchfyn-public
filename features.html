What StitchFYN actually does

You do not need more software. You need less chaos.

Fabric on record

Every fabric is recorded at intake and confirmed with the customer, from their own phone, or in person with you. Every change is signed and kept, so the record can't be quietly edited. The fabric is on record before a single stitch is cut.

Your December is manageable

StitchFYN shows you which orders are most urgent and flags the ones at risk of missing their date. You set how many orders you can handle, with a different limit for festive peaks, and StitchFYN warns you before you accept one order too many.

Evidence you can export

Export the order's evidence as one PDF: fabric record, brief, payments, production stages, messages and the seal.

Everything else your workshop runs on

Customer order link

Each customer gets a private link to follow every stage, see progress photos and message you. Fewer "is it ready?" calls.

Group & aso-ebi orders

Weddings and family events run as one group: fabric intake for every member, a lead tailor, and one shared link for the coordinator.

Deposits & payments

Every naira recorded against the order it belongs to. No more arguments about what was paid.

Staff with the right access

Invite your team by email or a link you send on WhatsApp. Tailors see only the work assigned to them.

Order messages on record

Conversations about an order stay with the order, attributed to whoever sent them.

StitchScore

Your on-time record and repeat customers, turned into a reputation score you can track. It appears once you have completed 10 orders.

Reports that answer real questions

Which stages slow you down, which tailor is fastest, what was paid and who still owes.

Customer's fabric or your stock

Take orders on fabric the customer brings, or on stock from your vault, which is reserved automatically.

Your own production stages

Set your own production stages for each kind of garment you make.

Customer profiles and measurements

Each customer's measurements are saved as versions, and a version is locked before an order uses it.

Tailors can take orders

An owner setting lets your tailors take orders too.

A customer link for WhatsApp

Staff can copy each customer's order link and send it on WhatsApp.

A signed log of every action

Every action is kept in a signed log you can view in the app.

Keeps working when your connection drops

StitchFYN was built for Nigerian workshops, not Silicon Valley offices. When your connection drops, stage updates and fabric photos wait on your phone and send themselves when you are back online.
