Part 1 Get started

1

Request and activate your workspace

Owner

What you see: Request a workspace, at app.stitchfyn.com/signup.

  1. Enter your full name, business name, email, WhatsApp number and country, then tap Request workspace. You see Request received.
  2. Our team reviews every request and replies by email. Once yours is approved, StitchFYN emails you a Verify email and set password link.
  3. The link opens Set your password. Type a password in Create a password; the checklist ticks off each rule: at least 8 characters, an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number.
  4. Type it again in Confirm password and click Set password & continue.

What happens next: you land on the sign-in page with the message "Email verified. Sign in with the password you just set." The emailed link expires after 24 hours.

2

Sign in

Owner Staff

What you see: the Welcome back sign-in card.

  1. Enter your email address and password.
  2. Click Sign In.

What happens next: the first time an owner signs in, StitchFYN opens the setup (step 3). After that, and for staff, you go straight to your dashboard. If you forget your password, click Forgot password? and StitchFYN emails you a reset link.

3

Finish the first-time setup

Owner

What you see: Set up followed by your business name, with four steps across the top: Your workshop, Production stages, Your team and Ready. Each step saves as you go, so you can leave and pick up where you stopped.

  1. Your workshop: enter the workshop address, how many garments you can have in production at once and your working hours a day. New orders warn you when you reach that limit.
  2. Production stages: click Use the standard stages to start with the standard sequence. You can rename, add or reorder stages later (step 5).
  3. Your team: to invite someone, enter their name and email, choose their role and click Send invite (more in step 7). Working alone? Click I work alone for now; you can invite staff whenever you're ready, and you can assign orders to yourself as Me (Owner).
  4. Ready: when every item is ticked, click Finish and take your first order.

What happens next: your workshop is ready for its first order. If something is still missing, the checklist names it and Do this takes you back to that step.

4

Read your dashboard

Owner

What you see: Workshop overview, with tiles for your workshop capacity, active orders, orders at risk of missing their date, completed orders and average completion time. Below are your store's capacity, your StitchScore and the latest Team activity.

  1. Use the green sidebar to move between areas: Orders, New Order, Production, Customers, Inventory, Workflow Templates, Staff Management, Reports, StitchScore, Settings and the Forensic Center. On a phone, the bar at the bottom holds the main areas and More holds the rest. The Forensic Center holds your workshop's full record of who did what, and when (step 20).
  2. Click the bell for notifications, and your initial (top right) to change your password or log out.

What happens next: the numbers update as your team works. StitchScore unlocks once you have completed 10 orders; until then it shows your progress towards it.

Part 2 Set up your workshop

5

Build production blueprints

Owner Manager

What you see: Workflow Templates in the sidebar. A blueprint is the list of production stages for one kind of garment, plus the measurements it needs.

  1. Click New Blueprint, give it a name (for example "Lace Gown") and a short description, then click Create Blueprint.
  2. Open it with the arrow on its card. New blueprints start with the standard sequence: Intake, Cutting, Sewing, Embroidery, Finishing, Quality Check and Ready for Collection, each with an estimated time in minutes.
  3. Under Required Measurements, click the pencil, type the points you always take separated by commas (Chest, Waist, Hips, Dress length…) and click Save.
  4. To add a stage, type its name and minutes under New stage name and Duration, click Add stage, then use the arrows to move it into place. Rename, retime or remove stages the same way, then click Save changes.

What happens next: the blueprint appears as a garment type when you create orders, and its stages become the production checklist for every garment made from it. Changes apply to new orders only; garments already in production keep their stages.

Tip: Ready for Collection is always the last stage, and Quality Check must come before it. The Quality Check stage always needs a photo before it can be completed.

6

Stock your fabric inventory

Owner Manager

What you see: Inventory, your stock of materials. Register the fabric you sell or keep in stock here. If your customers always bring their own fabric, you can skip this step.

  1. Click Register Material and take or choose a photo of the fabric. The photo is required.
  2. Enter the Material Name, an optional SKU / Reference, and the unit you count it in (Yards, Meters, Pieces or Boxes). Click Register Material.
  3. On the new row, click Receive Stock, enter the quantity that arrived and click Confirm Shipment.

What happens next: each material shows its Total, Reserved and Available stock. Fabric is reserved automatically when an order uses it.

7

Invite your team (optional)

Owner

What you see: Staff Management (Identity & Access), listing everyone with access to your workshop, including anyone you invited during setup. If you work alone, skip this step and come back when you take someone on.

  1. Click Invite Staff.
  2. Enter their full name and email, choose a System Role (Manager, Tailor or Front Desk Member) and the store they work in.
  3. Click Grant Access.

What happens next: they receive an email with a Set my password link, which opens the same page you used in step 1. StitchFYN also shows you the link, so you can copy it or share it on WhatsApp if the email doesn't arrive. It works once and expires in 7 days. From this page you can later resend an invite, change someone's role, suspend them or remove them; every change is recorded in the access log.

Who sees what: tailors see their Dashboard, My Orders and the Production board, and only the garments assigned to them. Front desk staff can take orders and manage clients.

Part 3 Take an order

8

Add a client

Owner Front desk

What you see: Customers, your client list with a search box.

  1. Click + New Client.
  2. Enter the client's full name and phone number (both required) and, if you have it, their email address.
  3. Click Create Profile.

What happens next: the client's profile opens, ready for measurements. If you added an email address, StitchFYN emails the client a welcome message.

9

Record measurements

Owner Front desk

What you see: the client profile, with contact details, Master Measurements, order history and the measurement version history.

  1. Click Record New Measurement (Initialize Profile the first time).
  2. Under Add custom precision point, type a point (for example "Chest") and press Enter or click Add Label. Repeat for each point.
  3. Enter each value in centimetres and click Commit Version.

What happens next: the measurements appear on the profile as a numbered version. Every later change creates a new version, so earlier measurements are never overwritten. Once the client has agreed a version, Lock & Seal Version locks it so nobody can change it. New orders reuse saved measurements only once they are locked.

10

Create the order

Owner Front desk

What you see: Production Intake, a guided form with numbered steps. Start it from New Order in the sidebar, or from Create New Order on a client's profile (which skips the client step).

  1. Client: search for an existing client and pick them, or click Add New Client Profile.
  2. Blueprint and event date: click a garment type (each click adds an item) and, if you like, choose who makes it under Assign Tailor: one of your tailors, or Me (Owner) if you sew it yourself. Pick the event date (the day the customer needs to wear it) and the store. Under Order classification choose the occasion, garment category and payment structure, enter the agreed price, choose the construction tier, then click Configure Material.
  3. Material: choose the fabric from your inventory (it shows how much is left), or choose Customer's own fabric if the customer brought it. Either way, enter the quantity and its unit, pick a colour swatch and add design notes. The fabric photo is taken on the order page once the order is saved; every garment needs its own.
  4. Measurements: if the client's locked measurements already cover what the blueprint needs, StitchFYN uses them and shows Proceed to Review. Otherwise click Capture Measurements, fill in the missing points and click Review Order.
  5. Review: check the summary and click Commit to Production.

What happens next: the order is saved and View Order opens the new order page. Fabric from your inventory is reserved for it automatically.

Part 4 Three checks before cutting

Production cannot start until the fabric and design brief are confirmed and the deposit is recorded (or the owner waives the deposit, with a reason on record). On the order page, under Status transitions, StitchFYN shows which are still outstanding: Deposit required, Fabric unconfirmed and Design pending.

11

Record the deposit

Owner Manager

What you see: the Payment Ledger card on the order page, with the price, what has been paid and the balance. A payment can only be recorded once the order has a price; if it has none yet, click Set price first.

  1. Click Add Payment.
  2. Enter the amount in naira, then choose the Payment Method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Card, POS or Other) and the Payment Intent (Deposit, Instalment, Final Payment or Full Upfront).
  3. Add a note such as the transfer reference, and click Record Payment.

What happens next: the payment appears in the ledger history, the paid amount and balance update, and the Deposit required flag clears. Only the owner or a manager can change the price later, with a note, and every change is kept in the price history.

12

Photograph and confirm the fabric

Owner Customer

What you see: on the order page's garment card, Fabric evidence photos and Fabric safety seal. Until the fabric is photographed, Confirm Fabric is greyed out with "Add a fabric photo first". This applies to every garment, whether the customer brought the fabric or it came from your inventory.

  1. Click Capture Fabric Photo. On a phone this opens the camera; photograph the fabric for this garment.
  2. If the customer is with you, click Confirm Fabric, check the recorded quantity with them, and click Confirm Fabric again. Otherwise the customer confirms from their own order page; until your photo arrives, their page says "Waiting for the workshop's photo of your fabric."

What happens next: StitchFYN issues a Fabric Safety Seal: a QR code and a verification code (for example FSS-ULYY-A92H) that anyone can check. Use Share via SMS to send it to the customer. Confirmation cannot be undone.

Working offline: a photo taken without signal waits on the phone and uploads when you're back online. Confirm Fabric stays greyed out until it has uploaded.

13

Agree the design brief

Owner Customer

What you see: the Design brief section of the order page.

  1. Click Create Design Brief and describe the silhouette, neckline, waist detail and back finish, with any additional notes.
  2. Click Create Brief. The brief shows Awaiting customer confirmation.
  3. The customer opens their private StitchFYN order page on their phone, reads the brief and taps Confirm Design Brief.

What happens next: once the fabric and brief are confirmed and the deposit is recorded (or waived by the owner, with a reason on record), the order is ready for production. No more "that's not what I asked for".

Part 5 Production

14

Assign a tailor and start production

Owner Manager

What you see: on the order page, Garment inventory, Production assignment and Status transitions. With all three checks done, no warning flags remain.

  1. Under Production assignment, choose the tailor in Assign Tailor, or Me (Owner) if you make it yourself. You can change the choice here later.
  2. Click → IN PRODUCTION.

What happens next: the garment moves to In production and appears on the Production board for the assigned tailor.

15

Work through the stages

Tailor Owner

What you see: Production, the production floor. Each row is a garment with its client, current stage, deadline and tailor. Use the stage chips and filters to narrow the list. Tailors see only the garments assigned to them, here and under My Orders.

  1. Click the Work on stage icon (the gold factory) on the garment's row. On a phone, tap Work on stage on its card.
  2. In Production stages, the active stage is outlined in green. Add an optional note and tap Add Progress Photo to photograph the work (up to 3 photos per stage).
  3. When the stage is done, click Mark Complete. The next stage becomes active.

What happens next: the customer's tracking page moves forward with every completed stage, and each completion is added to the order's record (step 18).

Working offline: if the workshop loses signal, stage updates are saved on the device and sent automatically when you're back online. Fabric photos taken offline are queued the same way.

16

Pass the quality check

Tailor

What you see: when the garment reaches the Quality Check stage, Mark Complete is greyed out with the note "Add at least one progress photo before completing this stage."

  1. Inspect the garment, then tap Add Progress Photo and photograph the finished piece.
  2. Click Mark Complete, then complete the last stage, Ready for Collection.

What happens next: the photo becomes part of the order's evidence, and the order is ready for the final review.

Part 6 Hand over with proof

17

Final quality review and collection

Owner Customer

What you see: at the bottom of the order page, Final Quality Review.

  1. Once every garment on the order has finished production, click Begin Collection. The review checklist appears: the garment matches the agreed brief, all fabric is accounted for, stitching and finishing are inspected, and the customer is satisfied.
  2. Go through the points with the garment in hand, then click Complete Quality Check.
  3. When the customer collects, they tap Confirm Collection on their order page.

What happens next: the order shows "You completed the final quality review", then the customer's confirmation, with dates on both.

18

Close the order and export evidence

Owner

What you see: the order header, with Export Evidence, Forensic Proof and Edit Order.

  1. Under Status transitions, click → COMPLETED. The garment status changes to Completed.
  2. Click Export Evidence to download a PDF of what StitchFYN recorded for the order: the fabric and its confirmation, the design brief, payments, each production stage and who completed it, messages and the seal code. The photos stay in StitchFYN; they are not in the PDF.
  3. Click Forensic Proof to see the order's full record, headed Immutable Audit Trail: every action, who did it and when. "Immutable" means nothing in it can be edited or deleted. Each entry also carries a signature (shown as SIG), a digital stamp that shows the entry has not been tampered with.

What happens next: the order counts towards your StitchScore, and its records can be checked at any time.

19

What your customer sees

Customer

Customers don't need an app or an account. Everything works in the phone's browser.

  • Tracking page: a greeting, the current stage, the delivery date and every stage of production, updated as your team works.
  • Order page: the order status, the production timeline with your team's progress photos, fabric and design confirmations, payments made, and a message thread with your workshop.
  • Seal check: anyone can confirm a Fabric Safety Seal by scanning its QR code or entering its code under Verify a Seal. It shows the fabric photo, tailor, business, date logged and quantity.
20

Check the audit log

Owner

What you see: Forensic Center → Audit Log, the System Audit Log for your whole workshop.

  1. Scroll the list. It covers company setup, fabric intake, payments, stage completions, photos and confirmations.
  2. Each entry shows who acted, the details, when it was recorded (Witnessed) and its signature.

What happens next: nothing can be edited or deleted here. If a customer ever disputes what happened, this record and the evidence PDF are your proof.
