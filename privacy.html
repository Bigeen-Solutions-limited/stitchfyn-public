Who we are

StitchFYN is built and run by Bigeen Solutions. You can reach us at info@bigeensolutions.com.

What we collect on this site

When you apply to be a Founding Tailor, we collect your full name, shop name, city, WhatsApp number, how long you have been tailoring and how many people work in your shop. Nothing else is collected through this page.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you on WhatsApp about the Founding Tailor programme and to set up your workshop on StitchFYN if you join. We do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your information. The application form is delivered to us through a form-processing service, which stores it on our behalf and for no other purpose.

How long we keep it

If you join, your details become part of your StitchFYN account. If you do not join, we delete your application within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you gave us at any time. Email info@bigeensolutions.com or tell us on WhatsApp, and we will act on it promptly.
