Privacy notice

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who we are

StitchFYN is built and run by Bigeen Solutions. You can reach us at info@bigeensolutions.com.

What we collect on this site

When you apply to be an Early Adopter, we collect your full name, business name, email address, country and WhatsApp number. Nothing else is collected through this page.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you on WhatsApp about the Early Adopter programme, to set up your workshop on StitchFYN if you join, and to email you the link to sign in to it. We do not use it for advertising.

Counting visits

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your information. The application form is received by Formspree, a protected form service that stores it on our behalf and for no other purpose.

How long we keep it

If you join, your details become part of your StitchFYN account. If you do not join, we delete your application within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you gave us at any time. Email info@bigeensolutions.com or tell us on WhatsApp, and we will act on it promptly.
