Segun

Auchi · Mid-range fashion designer, 3 staff

A customer claimed he swapped expensive Guinea Brocade for cheaper material. Segun never photographed the fabric. He paid ₦350,000 to make the police report go away.

A timestamped fabric record, confirmed by the customer, would have given him evidence to show.

Maryam

Kaduna · Final-year student, first-class convocation

She saved three months to buy her convocation gown fabric. The shop was locked when she came to collect. The number went to voicemail. She wore a borrowed dress to her own first-class ceremony.

The Fabric Safety Seal is issued the moment she confirms her fabric: a digital receipt anyone can check.
