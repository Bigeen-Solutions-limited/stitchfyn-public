How it works

From fabric drop-off to collection — every step on record

Four steps, the same for every order.

  1. 1

    Intake, recorded

    Record the fabric when the order is taken (a photo is part of the record). The customer confirms it from their own phone, or in person with you. A Fabric Safety Seal is issued the moment they confirm.

  2. 2

    Three checks before a single cut

    Production cannot start until the fabric and design brief are confirmed and the deposit is recorded (or the owner waives the deposit, with a reason on record). No more "that's not what I asked for".

  3. 3

    Production, one tap per stage

    Cutting, sewing, finishing — each tailor sees the garments assigned to them and moves each one forward in seconds. Quality control needs a photo before it can be signed off.

  4. 4

    Collection, with proof

    A final quality review, then collection is recorded. Your customer followed every stage, with progress photos, on their own order link, so nobody is surprised on the day.
